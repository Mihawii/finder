**Effective February 18, 2026, IRCC raised the minimum qualifying work experience for all renewed category-based draws from 6 months to 12 months.**

**IMPORTANT — NOC-based eligibility:** Category-based draws require work experience in specific NOC codes within the relevant sector. TEER level alone does NOT determine eligibility. When a candidate's specific occupation or sector is unknown, category-based draws should NOT be recommended.

## PROVINCIAL UPDATES (as of March 29, 2026)

### Alberta — AAIP
- **2026 allocation:** approx. 6,403 nominations.
- **Active worker streams:** Alberta Express Entry Stream, Alberta Opportunity Stream, Tourism & Hospitality Stream, Rural Renewal Stream.
- **Active entrepreneur streams:** Rural Entrepreneur, Graduate Entrepreneur, Farm Stream, Foreign Graduate Entrepreneur.
- **Dedicated pathways** within EE stream for healthcare professionals, technology workers, and police.
- **Recent changes:**
  - Nov 18, 2025: Rural Renewal Stream modifications.
  - Sep 9, 2025: Additional allocations approved.
  - Apr 1, 2025: PTE Core accepted; revised worker stream application fees.

### Ontario — OINP
- **2026 allocation:** 14,119 nominations (+31% vs ~10,750 in 2025; below 2024 peak).
- **Streams:** 9 immigration streams active.
- **Recent changes:**
  - As of Jul 2, 2025: Employer Portal required for employment position approvals (replacing previous submission methods).
  - Ontario Regulation 421/17 amended giving minister more flexibility to create/amend/close streams.
  - Program redesign ongoing — consolidation of employer job offer streams planned.
- **Current streams remain operational** pending redesign completion.

### British Columbia — BC PNP
- **2026 allocation:** 5,254 nominations (BC had requested 9,000).
- **Active streams:** Skills Immigration (Skilled Worker, Health Authority, ELSS), Express Entry BC, Entrepreneur Immigration.
- **Recent changes:**
  - Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctorate streams: **indefinitely suspended** since April 2025.
  - International Post-Graduate (IPG) stream: **officially closed** January 7, 2026.
  - Healthcare and Tech occupations explicitly prioritized.
- **2026 draw activity:** 5 entrepreneur draws + 2 Skills Immigration draws (Feb 4 & Feb 11, cumulative 889 ITAs) as of early March 2026.

### Saskatchewan — SINP
- **2026 allocation:** approx. 4,761 nominations.
- **Structure:** Moving toward sector-based allocation model.
- **Priority sectors:** healthcare, agriculture, trades, manufacturing, energy, tech.
- **Note:** Detailed stream-level updates not available from hub page; specific sub-pages would need to be checked for draw results and intake windows.
