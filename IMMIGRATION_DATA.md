# Immigration Program Data — PathwayFinder (Static)
## Last Updated: March 24, 2026

### Scope
This file covers **economic immigration and Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) pathways only**.

**NOT covered:**
Quebec immigration (separate system), family sponsorship, caregiver programs, refugee/asylum/H&C, Start-Up Visa, Self-Employed, Agri-Food Pilot, Rural & Northern Immigration Pilot, Francophone Community Pilot, International Experience Canada (IEC), temporary public policies.

### Accuracy Notes
- Provincial program timelines and future changes are **based on current public announcements and program guides; they can change without notice**.
- Costs are **approximate typical ranges** — always verify current amounts on official federal or provincial websites before advising.
- Settlement fund amounts are **updated at least annually by IRCC** — verify against the current IRCC table.
- This file should be checked against official sources every 2–4 weeks.

## FEDERAL PROGRAMS

### Federal Skilled Worker Program (FSW)
- Status: **ACTIVE** (Express Entry–managed).
- Core requirements (summary; see IRCC for full rules):
  - At least **1 year** of continuous full-time (or equivalent) skilled work experience in TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3 within the last 10 years.
  - **Language:** minimum CLB 7 in all abilities on an approved test.
  - **Education:** at least secondary school; foreign credentials generally require an **ECA**.
  - **Selection grid:** minimum **67/100** points on the FSW selection factors grid.
  - **Settlement funds** (unless exempt due to valid job offer / Canadian status).
  - Admissibility (medical, security, etc.).

**FSW 67-point selection grid breakdown:**
| Factor | Maximum Points |
|--------|---------------|
| Education | 25 |
| First official language | 28 (CLB 7=4pts, CLB 8=5pts, CLB 9+=6pts per ability) |
| Second official language | 4 (CLB 5+=1pt per ability) |
| Work experience | 15 (1yr=9, 2-3yr=11, 4-5yr=13, 6+yr=15) |
| Age | 12 (18-35=12pts, -1pt per year over 35, 0 at 47+) |
| Arranged employment | 10 |
| Adaptability | 10 |
| **Total** | **100 (minimum 67 required)** |

- Selection and invitations:
  - Candidates enter the **Express Entry pool** and receive a CRS score.
  - **CRS cutoffs vary by draw.** There is no fixed FSW "program cutoff"; recent CEC-oriented draws in early 2026 have landed around CRS 507–511 but this is not a rule.

### Canadian Experience Class (CEC)
- Status: **ACTIVE** — one of the most frequently used categories in 2026.
- Core requirements (summary):
  - At least **1 year** of skilled Canadian work experience (TEER 0–3) in the **last 3 years**.
  - **Language:**
    - TEER 0–1 work: at least CLB 7
    - TEER 2–3 work: at least CLB 5
  - **No settlement funds** required.
  - ECA **not mandatory** if education is not being used to claim CRS points (education still affects CRS).
- Selection and invitations:
  - Candidates enter the **Express Entry** pool and compete based on CRS.
  - Recent CEC draws in early 2026 have had cutoffs around **507–511**, but actual CRS thresholds change each draw.

### Federal Skilled Trades Program (FST)
- Status: **ACTIVE** (Express Entry–managed).
- Core requirements (summary):
  - At least **2 years** of full-time work experience in a skilled trade (eligible NOC codes) within the last 5 years.
  - **Language:** about CLB 5 for speaking/listening and CLB 4 for reading/writing (verify exact bands).
  - Either:
    - A valid **job offer** in Canada (full-time, at least 1 year) in the skilled trade, or
    - A valid **certificate of qualification** in that skilled trade from a Canadian authority.
  - Admissibility.
- CRS:
  - FST candidates join the Express Entry pool and are invited based on CRS when IRCC holds relevant draws.
  - CRS cutoffs for FST-specific draws have historically been lower than general draws, but vary each time.

### Study-to-PR Pathway (common route, not a formal "program")
This is a commonly used route, **not** an official IRCC program name:
> Study Permit → Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) → Canadian Work Experience → Express Entry/PNP → PR

Key 2024–2026 policies (summarized; always verify current IRCC guidance):
- **Study permit caps and program limits:** IRCC has introduced national and regional caps; these can change yearly.
- **PGWP new language requirements** (effective November 1, 2024, per IRCC official guidance):
  - University degree graduates (bachelor's, master's, PhD): CLB/NCLC 7 in all four abilities.
  - Most college/diploma/certificate graduates: CLB/NCLC 5 in all four abilities.
  - Source: canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html
- **Field-of-study requirement:** For many non-degree programs (study permit applied on/after Nov 1, 2024), the field must align with priority labour-shortage areas. University degree graduates (bachelor's, master's, doctoral) are exempt from field-of-study requirement.
- Always rely on the current PGWP eligibility page on IRCC before advising.

- 2026 provincial nomination allocation: **14,119** (roughly +31% vs ~10,750 in 2025; lower than the 2024 peak).

- Ontario has announced and begun implementing a **major redesign** of its PNP; some details are still evolving.

Current reality (as of March 24, 2026):

- Analysts expect greater focus on **healthcare, high-demand occupations, and exceptional talent**, but **exact future streams and end-dates for current streams are not all finalized**.

### Saskatchewan — SINP (Saskatchewan Immigrant Nominee Program)
- 2026 allocation: approx. **4,761**.
- Application fee: about **$350**.
- Saskatchewan is moving toward a more **sector-based allocation model**.

High-level structure:
- A portion of nominations is reserved for **priority sectors** (e.g., healthcare, agriculture, trades, manufacturing, energy, tech).
- Some sectors (e.g., food service, trucking, certain retail) may operate with caps or intake windows.
- Non-economic streams like entrepreneur/farm programs have been scaled back or closed in some cases.

Program notes:
- Some restrictions apply to certain temporary residents (e.g., spousal open work permit holders, or PGWP holders not working in Saskatchewan) under specific sub-streams. Exact wording must be taken from the current SINP guide.

### Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP)
- Type: **Atlantic regional federal program**, not a PNP.
- Provinces: **New Brunswick (NB), Nova Scotia (NS), Prince Edward Island (PE), Newfoundland & Labrador (NL)**.
- Approximate 2026 federal allocation: around **4,000** spots (down from earlier years; verify each year).
- Core model:
  - Employer must be **designated** under AIP.
  - Candidate needs a **valid job offer** in an eligible occupation.
  - Settlement plans and employer support are required.

Recent practical notes:
- Some provinces (notably NB) have periodically paused new **employer designations**.
- Certain sectors (e.g., some food service roles) may have tighter controls or quotas.

Always consult the **AIP page for each Atlantic province** plus IRCC's federal AIP page for current rules.

### Nova Scotia — NSNP (Nova Scotia Nominee Program)
- 2026 provincial allocation: not yet fully published; part of the national PNP target.
- Program fee: varies by stream.

As of early 2026, Nova Scotia has **consolidated multiple streams into four main ones**:

| New Stream               | Former Streams Included (examples)                               |
|--------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Skilled Worker           | Skilled Worker, some pilots (e.g., construction), Physicians, Occupations in Demand |
| Nova Scotia Graduate     | International Graduates in Demand (renamed/expanded)            |
| Entrepreneur             | Entrepreneur, International Graduate Entrepreneur                |
| Nova Scotia: Express Entry | NS Experience: EE, Labour Market Priorities, LMP for Physicians |

General requirement patterns (high-level):
- Skilled Worker: full-time job offer from a NS employer; experience and language requirements vary by TEER level.
- Express Entry: active EE profile, LOI from NSNP, minimum language consistent with EE program.
- Graduate: NS education + work experience + valid status.
- Physician pathways: job offer from NSHA/IWK, often with return-of-service commitments.
